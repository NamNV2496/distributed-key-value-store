Where does a request go?

A key is hashed to a slot, the slot belongs to a shard, and the shard's leader executes the write.

Slot ownership

Every hash slot has exactly one owning shard. Width is the share of the keyspace.

Shards & their nodes

One Raft group per shard. The crowned node is the elected leader; followers replicate from it.

Slots in migration

Writes to these slots are rejected with a retry hint until the copy finishes.
